Installing (sideload)

1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap a Download APK.
2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask to allow installing from this source — tap Settings → Allow from this source, then Install.
3. Launch the app — it shows ● Connected when it reaches the API. Tap 📷 Scan with camera to scan QR codes, or type/paste them.
Release-signed builds (TrackNTrash certificate). Sideloading outside the Play Store still asks each phone to allow installs from your browser once.
